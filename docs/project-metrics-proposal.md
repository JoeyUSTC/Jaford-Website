# Project evidence and cumulative metrics — proposal

Not approved for publication. No aggregate counters are rendered on the website.

## Suggested homepage placement

Hero → featured project stories → Custom R&D capabilities → standard product sectors → contact. Once the owner approves verified totals, add a restrained three-column metrics strip alongside the story section. No animated counters or implied success rate.

## Recommended initial measures

| Public label | Counting rule | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Projects completed | One unique scoped engagement with an accepted final delivery or validation report. Multiple purchases, batches or case articles within that engagement do not create additional projects. An unexpected scientific result can still complete an agreed validation project. | Unique internal project ID, agreed scope, completion date and delivery/acceptance record. |
| Research cells delivered | Actual custom research cells handed over, with dry and formed cells separately recorded. Exclude orders, targets, standard commercial cells and replacements counted twice. | Final delivered quantities and cell state per project. |
| Synthesized materials delivered | Net delivered synthesized output, normalized to kg internally. Exclude feedstock, purchased standard materials and intermediate batches counted again in final output. Show material-family subtotals if useful. | Output material identity, final accepted mass, unit and delivery record. |

A case article count is not the company's total project count. Current stories alone are not a complete ledger. Do not infer final cell quantities from initial requests, or carbon output from resin feedstock. Do not publish a cumulative figure until records are reconciled.

## Possible later measures

- Testing: completed agreed test campaigns, grouped by electrochemical testing and characterization. Do not mix samples, charge/discharge cycles, SEM images or repeat measurements into one total.
- Standard supply: fulfilled orders or completed deliveries, counted separately from development projects. A product listing does not prove a fulfilled order. Include only verified records; no invented delivery stories.
- Lead time: median calendar days from agreed scope/material readiness to accepted delivery, by comparable service type. State sample size and measurement window. Avoid cumulative project duration or promised turnaround based on one example.
- Estimated research spend avoided: only where an attributable, documented alternative quotation matches scope, quantity, quality, timing and currency. Subtract actual total delivered cost on the same basis. Do not treat unused budget as savings, extrapolate from examples, or expose private pricing. Keep off the homepage until this comparison is defensible and publication is approved.

## Ledger and updates

Keep raw financial, customer and acceptance records private, outside this public repository. Suggested fields: internal project ID, public case code (optional), sector, scope type, status, acceptance date, final output quantity/unit, output state/material family, supporting record reference, publication permission, metric eligibility and exclusion reason. Summaries should state an as-of date and be updated monthly after reconciliation.
