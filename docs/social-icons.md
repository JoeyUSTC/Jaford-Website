# Social logo provenance

The owner requested placeholders for Twitter, LinkedIn, Taobao, WeChat Official Account and Douyin. Account URLs and the WeChat destination/QR have not been supplied. The website displays non-interactive marks with accessible English names and hover titles after the contact forms on the homepage and independent contact page. No platform homepages, fake URLs or additional channels are used.

`src/social-icons.js` bundles unmodified vector path data from [Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0), distributed under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/LICENSE.md). Brand marks remain subject to their respective trademark rights. The Douyin placeholder uses the shared musical-note silhouette from the TikTok icon; its accessible name and future destination are Douyin, not a TikTok account.

Sources fetched from the pinned package:

- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/icons/twitter.svg`
- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/icons/linkedin.svg`
- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/icons/taobao.svg`
- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/icons/wechat.svg`
- `https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11.15.0/icons/tiktok.svg`

These are build-time source references only. The visitor's browser makes no requests to a third-party icon service, social network or shop.
