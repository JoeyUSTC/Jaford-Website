# Hard Carbon precursor-route guide

Owner-authorized 2026-11-09. Only the existing Hard Carbon family page gains a material-options list. Other materials, catalog models, specification registries, PDFs and URLs remain unchanged. The six entries illustrate precursor routes within the owner's resin/biomass sourcing scope; they are not six confirmed supplier products or fixed acceptance specifications.

## Public content and boundaries

Phenolic-resin, furan-resin, coconut-shell, nutshell, lignin and cellulose routes each have a brief table: precursor family, typical precursor, research focus and selection considerations. Walnut shell is the example behind the broader nutshell label. Particle size, ash/mineral residues, precursor provenance and thermal history are discussion/selection factors, not promised values or available customization limits. No supplier identity, grade code, price, stock, capacity, first-cycle efficiency, BET value, tolerance or lead time is invented. No paper's measured result is transferred to a JAFORD offering.

The introduction identifies these as route profiles, with supplier grades/batch specifications matched to each project. The interface says Material profile, with no Spec/PDF placeholder. A future verified supplier datasheet can be added per grade after identity, provenance and batch applicability are checked. Existing legacy PDF URLs remain intact and public model/Spec UI remains paused elsewhere.

## Research references

- *Review: Insights on Hard Carbon Materials for Sodium-Ion Batteries: Synthesis–Properties–Performance Relationships*, Advanced Energy Materials, DOI [10.1002/aenm.202303833](https://doi.org/10.1002/aenm.202303833). Publisher-indexed precursor overview supports phenolic resin, cellulose and lignin as research feedstocks; no numeric results are reproduced.
- *A review on biomass-derived hard carbon materials for sodium-ion batteries*, Materials Advances (2021), DOI [10.1039/D1MA00315A](https://doi.org/10.1039/D1MA00315A). The publisher-indexed overview supports plant-shell and biopolymer routes; direct full-text fetching returned 403 during this review.
- *Hard carbon derived from coconut shells, walnut shells, and corn silk biomass waste exhibiting high capacity for Na-ion batteries*, Journal of Energy Chemistry (2021), [author manuscript](https://hal.science/hal-03320889/file/C%20Nita%20et%20al.%20-%20J%20Energ%20Chem%202021-HAL.pdf). Indexed manuscript text identifies coconut/walnut feedstocks. The site's profiles omit the paper's numerical performance and process conditions.
- *Preparation of Enriched Closed-Pores Hard Carbon for High-Performance Sodium-Ion Batteries by the Poly(vinyl butyral) Template Method* (2024), DOI [10.1021/acsaem.4c00331](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsaem.4c00331), publisher-indexed abstract; and [WO2019046813A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2019046813A1/en), indexed precursor discussion. These support furan/furfuryl precursor terminology only, not reproduction of a patented process or any claimed JAFORD performance.

References remain in internal documentation; customer navigation does not lead offsite to research papers. Supplier confirmation is still required before presenting grade-specific datasheets.
